Hawaiʻi Volcanoes

Three seconds.
Then it holds still.

One clip, one file, one crop, fetched whether you scroll to it or not.

One replaces the other.

Four frames, swapped with no transition at all, and the same file served to a phone as to a desktop.

It opens as you arrive.

Except that it does not: the panel is the shape it will always be.

Dealt out, not flashed in.

All three arrive at once, which is the same as none of them arriving.
